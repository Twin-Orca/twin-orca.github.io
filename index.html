Why Twin Orca exists

Everything costs something now.
Usually your money, and always your data.

Simple tools have turned into subscriptions. Free apps quietly collect your habits, your spending and your health, then sell or leak them. Twin Orca is the other way: build the tool properly, give it to everyone, and keep every byte on the device it belongs to.

Orcas hunt in pods: small, sharp, working together. So do these apps. Each one does one job well, and together they cover the everyday things you track, like money and workouts.
