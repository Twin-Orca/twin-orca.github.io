Training Arc – Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Published by Twin Orca

The short version

Training Arc does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you log stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Your workouts, exercises, sets, bodyweight, split and settings are saved in a local database on your device. Twin Orca has no server and never receives this data.

No accounts, no tracking

  • No sign-up or login.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.

Export and import

You can export your data as a CSV file. The file is created only when you tap Export, and you choose where it goes using your phone's share sheet. Importing reads a CSV file you pick. Twin Orca never sees these files.

Permissions

The app only uses vibration, for haptic feedback. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all your data permanently. Export a CSV first if you want a backup.

Children

Training Arc is not directed at children under 13.

Health note

Training Arc is a workout log, not medical advice. Check with a professional before starting a new training programme.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
