Sea Spends – Privacy Policy

Developer: Twin Orca · Effective date: 4 November 2026

Sea Spends is a personal income and expense log for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

Sea Spends does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or financial data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Data you enter

The amounts, categories, dates and notes you log are saved only in a local database on your device. Twin Orca has no access to them. The app has no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync.

Data we collect

None. Sea Spends contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Exporting and importing

You can choose to export your entries as a CSV file, save it to a folder, or share it to another app such as Google Drive or email. This only happens when you start it, and the file goes only where you send it. Once shared, that file is handled under the policy of the app or service you chose. Importing reads a CSV file you select and saves its entries on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in the app. Uninstalling Sea Spends or clearing its storage removes all of your data from the device permanently. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it.

Children

Sea Spends is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
